## KOC.SA.002 — Safe Control and Use of Scaffolding, Portable Ladders and Stepladders (21 items)

**Q001** · *Medium* · Scaffolding  
According to KOC.SA.002, how often must an in-service scaffold be re-inspected by an authorized Scaffolding Inspector, whether or not it has been used?  
A. Every 7 days  
B. Every 14 days  
C. Every 30 days  
D. Only after alterations or bad weather  
*Key: A — KOC.SA.002 Cl. 4.1.3 / 5.1.6: inspection before first use, after alteration, after adverse weather and every 7 days thereafter.*

**Q002** · *Medium* · Scaffolding

A. 40 km/h  
B. 50 km/h  
C. 65 km/h  
D. 80 km/h  
*Key: C — KOC.SA.002 Cl. 5.1.3 c): work on scaffolds is prohibited during storms or high winds (wind speed more than 65 km/h).*
