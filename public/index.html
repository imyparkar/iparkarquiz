How it works

  1. 1

    Choose topics and level

    Pick any of the 24 KOC HSSE procedures and a difficulty — Easy for direct recall, Medium for workplace scenarios and specific limits, or a mix of both.

  2. 2

    Share the QR code

    Project the join screen. People scan the code, or open the link you share by WhatsApp or email, and type their name.

  3. 3

    Play, review and score

    Each question runs for 40 seconds. After it closes, everyone sees the correct answer and the procedure reference, and the live leaderboard updates.

What’s in the question bank

The quiz draws up to 20 questions from a bank of 500, spread evenly across the procedures you choose. Answer options are shuffled every run, so the same session never plays twice in the same order. Correct answers stay on the server until each question closes.
